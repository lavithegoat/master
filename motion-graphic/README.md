# Finding My Voice — Personal Growth Motion Graphic

A ~4.5-minute Vox-style mixed-media motion graphic built from Precious's
personal SWOT / growth reflection narration (Nottingham Trent University,
MSc Human Resource Management).

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1280x720)
- **Audio:** the original recorded narration plays throughout, split into
  27 aligned 10-second blocks.
- **Visual style:** editorial paper-collage (archival cutouts, halftone,
  hand-drawn marker annotations) via Higgsfield `gemini_omni`.
- **Through-line:** a coral paper speech bubble that starts crumpled and
  tiny, grows across the story, and becomes a megaphone at the finale —
  visualising the journey from fear of speaking to finding her voice.

## Files
- `transcript.txt` — the narration script
- `captions.srt` — timed captions (load alongside the video in any player)
- `prompts.md` — the 27 scene prompts (one per 10s block)
- `blocks.txt` — narration split per 10s block
- `manifest.json` — block → clip/audio media-id mapping
